📖 Theory

What is Cheminformatics?

Cheminformatics combines chemistry, computer science and information technology to store, search, visualize and analyze chemical information.

What is SMILES?

SMILES means Simplified Molecular Input Line Entry System. It represents a chemical structure using text characters.

Examples: Water: O | Ethanol: CCO | Benzene: c1ccccc1

Molecular Descriptors

Descriptors are numerical or textual properties used to describe molecules, such as molecular weight, formula, hydrogen-bond donors and acceptors.
